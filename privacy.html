Privacy Policy for Pixel Logic

Last updated: October 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Pixel Logic, developed by Ebubekir Kaygısız, collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the Pixel Logic mobile application.

By using Pixel Logic, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Account Use and Guest Mode

Pixel Logic can be used without signing in. Users may also choose to sign in with a Google account to enable cloud save and account-based features.

When you use Pixel Logic without signing in, game progress and certain settings may be stored locally on your device. Local-only data may be lost if you uninstall the app, clear the app's storage, or reset your device.

2. Google Sign-In and Firebase Authentication

Pixel Logic uses Google Sign-In together with Firebase Authentication to provide optional account functionality.

When you sign in, Pixel Logic may process:

  • Your Firebase user identifier (UID)
  • Your Google account email address
  • Basic authentication information required to maintain your session

Pixel Logic does not receive or store your Google account password. Authentication is handled by Google and Firebase Authentication.

3. Cloud Save Data

If you sign in and use cloud save, Pixel Logic may store game-related data in Firebase Firestore so your progress can be restored after reinstalling the app or using another supported device.

Cloud save data may include:

  • Onboarding completion status
  • Current game and campaign progress
  • Completed level identifiers
  • Completed artwork and collection information
  • Game statistics
  • Daily challenge and streak information
  • Natural hint and life state
  • Hint and life regeneration timestamps
  • Technical cloud synchronization metadata

This information is used only to provide, synchronize, restore, secure, and improve the Pixel Logic gameplay experience.

4. Paid Hint Wallet and Supabase

Pixel Logic uses Supabase as a secure backend for purchased hint balances and related purchase-delivery operations.

The Supabase backend may process:

  • Your Firebase UID as an account identifier
  • Your remaining purchased hint balance
  • Paid-hint spending records used to prevent duplicate deductions
  • Technical purchase verification and delivery records

Pixel Logic does not create a separate user-facing Supabase account for you. Your Pixel Logic identity remains associated with your Firebase-authenticated account.

5. Google Play Purchases

Pixel Logic offers optional in-app purchases, including hint packages and other premium features.

Payments are processed by Google Play. Pixel Logic does not receive or store your full payment card information.

To securely verify and deliver eligible purchases, Pixel Logic's backend may communicate with the Google Play Developer API and process technical purchase information such as:

  • Product identifier
  • Google Play purchase token
  • Purchase status
  • Order or transaction status where applicable
  • Test-purchase status
  • Grant and consumption status

Sensitive server credentials and private keys are not embedded in or exposed by the Pixel Logic mobile application.

6. Fraud Prevention and Technical Purchase Records

When an account is deleted, Pixel Logic removes account-linked cloud data as described below. However, limited technical purchase information may be retained when reasonably necessary to prevent fraud, abuse, duplicate purchase grants, or to meet legal or security obligations.

Retained technical records may include:

  • An irreversible hash derived from a purchase token
  • Product identifier
  • Amount or entitlement granted
  • Grant or consumption state
  • Relevant technical timestamps

After account deletion, these records are designed to no longer contain the deleted account's Firebase UID, email address, raw purchase token, or order identifier where those fields are not required for security purposes.

7. Advertising

Pixel Logic may display advertisements using Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). Advertising services may process information such as device identifiers, app interactions, approximate location, diagnostics, and other technical information according to Google's policies and the consent choices available to you.

Pixel Logic uses Google's consent tools where required and requests ads only when the applicable consent and platform requirements allow it.

8. Analytics, Diagnostics, and App Performance

Google services and other integrated platform services may process diagnostic, app-interaction, crash, and performance information to maintain security, measure app performance, prevent fraud, and improve reliability.

9. Notifications

Pixel Logic may schedule local game reminders and other optional notifications if you grant notification permission.

Notification preferences can be managed within Pixel Logic and through your device settings.

10. How We Use Information

Information processed by Pixel Logic may be used to:

  • Provide and maintain the game
  • Authenticate users
  • Synchronize and restore game progress
  • Verify and deliver purchases
  • Maintain purchased hint balances
  • Prevent duplicate purchase grants, fraud, and abuse
  • Display and manage advertising
  • Maintain app security and reliability
  • Diagnose crashes and performance issues
  • Provide notifications requested by the user

11. Service Providers

Pixel Logic relies on third-party service providers to operate certain features. These may include:

  • Google Firebase Authentication
  • Google Firebase Firestore
  • Google Play Billing
  • Google Play Developer API
  • Google Mobile Ads / AdMob
  • Google User Messaging Platform where applicable
  • Supabase

These providers process information according to their own privacy, security, and data-protection practices.

12. Data Security

Pixel Logic uses reasonable technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect user information.

Network communication with Firebase, Supabase, Google Play, and other supported services uses encrypted HTTPS/TLS connections.

No method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

13. Data Selling

Pixel Logic does not sell your personal information to advertisers.

Advertising and analytics providers may process information as described in this Privacy Policy and according to their own policies.

14. Account and Data Deletion

If you create a Pixel Logic account using Google Sign-In, you can request deletion of your account and associated cloud data.

Delete your account inside Pixel Logic:
Settings → Google / Cloud Save account section → Delete Account & Cloud Data

Account deletion removes account-linked data including:

  • Your Firebase Authentication account
  • Your Pixel Logic cloud game progress
  • Your cloud collection and statistics
  • Your account-linked streak and gameplay state
  • Your Supabase paid-hint wallet
  • Your account-linked paid-hint spending records

Limited anonymized or minimized technical purchase records may remain where reasonably necessary for fraud prevention, duplicate-grant prevention, security, or legal compliance.

You can also request deletion without access to the app by visiting:

Pixel Logic Account & Data Deletion

15. Google Play Purchases After Account Deletion

Deleting your Pixel Logic account does not automatically erase Google's transaction history, issue refunds, or cancel subscriptions managed by Google Play.

Active subscriptions and purchases may need to be managed separately through Google Play.

16. Data Retention

Account-linked cloud data is retained while needed to provide your Pixel Logic account and cloud-save features, unless you request deletion.

Certain minimized technical records may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary for fraud prevention, security, abuse prevention, dispute handling, or legal compliance.

17. Users Under 18

Pixel Logic is intended for users aged 18 and over.

18. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Pixel Logic features, service providers, legal requirements, or data practices change.

The latest version will be available on this page with an updated date.
