Pixel Logic – Account & Data Deletion

Last updated: October 27, 2026

Pixel Logic provides users with ways to request deletion of their Pixel Logic account and associated cloud data.

You can delete your account directly inside the application or request deletion by email if you no longer have access to the app.

Method 1 – Delete Your Account in Pixel Logic

The fastest way to delete your Pixel Logic account is from inside the application.

  1. Open Pixel Logic.
  2. Sign in to the Google account linked to your Pixel Logic account.
  3. Open Settings.
  4. Find the Google / Cloud Save account section.
  5. Tap Delete Account & Cloud Data.
  6. Review and confirm the deletion request.
  7. Complete Google reauthentication if requested.
Account deletion is permanent.
Deleted Pixel Logic cloud progress and purchased cloud hint balances cannot be restored through Pixel Logic after deletion.

Method 2 – Request Deletion Without the App

If you cannot access Pixel Logic, you can request account and data deletion by contacting the developer.

Email: skyg66210@gmail.com

Request Account Deletion

In your request, provide only the minimum information necessary to identify the Pixel Logic account, such as the Google account email used with Pixel Logic.

Do not send:

  • Your Google password
  • Your payment card information
  • Your Google Play purchase token
  • Other sensitive credentials

We may ask for reasonable verification to prevent unauthorized deletion of another person's account.

Data Deleted With Your Pixel Logic Account

When your account deletion request is completed, Pixel Logic deletes account-linked data including:

  • Your Firebase Authentication account
  • Your cloud game progress
  • Your completed-level cloud data
  • Your cloud collection and artwork progress
  • Your account-linked statistics and streak information
  • Your Supabase purchased-hint wallet
  • Your account-linked paid-hint spending records
  • Account-specific cloud synchronization metadata

Data That May Be Retained

Pixel Logic may retain a limited amount of minimized technical data when reasonably necessary for security, fraud prevention, abuse prevention, duplicate purchase-grant prevention, dispute handling, or legal compliance.

This may include:

  • An irreversible hash derived from a Google Play purchase token
  • The purchased product identifier
  • The amount or entitlement previously granted
  • Technical grant or consumption state
  • Relevant technical timestamps

After account deletion, retained anti-fraud records are designed not to contain the deleted account's Firebase UID, email address, raw purchase token, or Google Play order ID where those identifiers are not required for security or legal purposes.

Google Play Purchases and Subscriptions

Deleting your Pixel Logic account does not automatically cancel, refund, or erase purchases or subscriptions maintained by Google Play.

If you have an active Google Play subscription, you may need to manage or cancel it separately through Google Play.

Google Play transaction records are controlled by Google and are subject to Google's own policies and legal obligations.

Guest / Local-Only Data

If you use Pixel Logic without signing in, some gameplay information may exist only on your device.

Local-only data can normally be removed by clearing Pixel Logic's app storage or uninstalling the application.

Deletion Requests

We aim to process valid account deletion requests as soon as reasonably possible after any necessary account verification.

Certain minimized technical records may be retained where necessary for security, fraud prevention, legal obligations, or similar legitimate purposes.
